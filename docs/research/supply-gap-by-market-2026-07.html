The supply gap, market by market

Research session 3 · gopher supply vs requestor demand, Jul 2025 – Jun 2026 · companion to sessions 1 (usage trends) and 2 (churn signals)

Headline: demand grew 28% while the active gopher fleet shrank 32%. In July 2025, 143 gophers delivered 474 orders (39% of everything placed). By June 2026, 97 gophers delivered 279 (18%). The platform loses roughly one in three delivering gophers per year while requestor demand accelerates — every growth win on the demand side (session 1) currently converts to expirations (session 2), not revenue.

What to do with this

  • Priority 1 — win-back-linked markets: Claremore OK, Conway AR, Marietta GA, New Britain CT, Portland OR. Each pairs a session-2 lapsed heavy spender with a measurable gap. Re-activate benched gophers there first (push/SMS “orders are going unfilled in your area”), then recruit; only after supply exists, send the session-2 win-back message.
  • Priority 2 — de-risk the one-gopher towns before they become next quarter’s decliner list: a second gopher in each of the five 100%-dependence markets is cheap insurance on ~$15k/yr of delivered volume.
  • Priority 3 — investigate gopher churn itself. The fleet shrank 143→97 while demand boomed; that’s unusual and worth its own session: payout economics, decline reasons, and what the 46 lost gophers have in common (a natural session 4).
  • Measure it going forward: the Deals ZIP-coverage tracker pattern in the HQ Dashboard could carry a “fill-rate by market” tile fed by this same Orders.csv pipeline.

Method

  • Market = drop-off city + state (pickup as fallback); 260 markets had ≥12 orders in the window; internal/test requestor accounts excluded.
  • Active gopher = delivered ≥1 order in the period (monthly series counts distinct gophers per month, so seasonality in participation shows).
  • Fill rate = delivered ÷ placed, by month the order was created. Cancelled orders count in the denominator (they are demand), which is why fill + expiry ≠ 100%.
  • Gophers needed = expired orders/month ÷ market per-gopher monthly delivery pace (floor: half the platform median of 2.95 deliveries/gopher/month). It sizes steady-state coverage, not launch-week blitz.
  • Bench counts non-deleted accounts with a Gopher role by address_state; “ever delivered” uses the received_orders counter in Users.csv.
