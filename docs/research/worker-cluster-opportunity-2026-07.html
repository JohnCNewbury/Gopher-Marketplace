The headline. 20,269 new requesters arrived in the last six months and placed 10,603 requests — but only 1,702 (16%) were delivered. 4,231 (40%) expired with no gopher ever accepting them. Nationwide only 303 gophers completed a single delivery in the entire window. Demand is not the constraint; workers are. Raleigh-Durham is the one market with real coverage (120 working gophers, 18% expiry) and it is the benchmark used below: ~11 requests per working gopher.

What this says to do

  • Activation before recruitment. 2,645 people signed up as gophers in the window; only 718 (27%) finished payout verification and only 303 ever delivered. In most target markets there is already a bench larger than the gap — Minneapolis has 29 signups, 7 verified, 0 working. Converting existing signups is cheaper than buying new ones.
  • Biggest absolute prize: New York. 849 new requesters and 429 requests against 11 working gophers — the largest demand pool on the platform with effectively no supply.
  • Fastest credibility win: the zero-fulfillment list. Minneapolis-St. Paul, Seattle-Tacoma and Baltimore each have 95–150 requests and literally zero deliveries. A handful of activated workers flips these from 0% to a working market.
  • Protect what works. Raleigh-Durham is 12% of all requests and 41% of all deliveries. Its 18% expiry rate is the proof the model works when supply is present.

Method & caveats

  • Window 2026-01-20 → 2026-07-20 from Dashboard/data/master/Users.csv and Orders.csv. Requesters counted by created_at + home ZIP; requests by CREATED AT + dropoff ZIP (pickup ZIP as fallback).
  • ZIPs geocoded from GeoNames US.txt centroids; clusters built greedily — busiest unassigned ZIP seeds a cluster and absorbs every unassigned ZIP within 25 miles. Metro labels come from the ZIP→DMA crosswalk.
  • Test/internal accounts excluded (@gophergo.io, "test", "placeholder") plus johnnewbury@sbcglobal.net — same rule as the earlier research sessions. That removed 8.4% of in-window signups and 126 orders concentrated in Holly Springs.
  • "Working gophers" = distinct GOPHER ID on a delivered order in the window — actual delivered supply, not registrations. "Workers needed" applies the Raleigh ratio (~11 requests per working gopher) to current volume — it sizes the gap for today's demand and does not model the additional demand better coverage would unlock.
  • Cluster counts are radius-bound: a metro wider than 50 miles appears as several clusters in the first table. Use the metro rollup for market-level totals.
