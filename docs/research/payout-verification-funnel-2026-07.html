The answer, in one line. Gophers do not fail to reach payout-verified because they lose interest — 70% of them are stopped by two documented, still-open product defects. 4,525 are hard-blocked at email confirmation (G40-271, Highest priority, open) and 2,975 reach Stripe but are dropped into the wrong onboarding flow (G40-13, Low priority, open since Aug 2024). Nothing else in the funnel loses meaningful volume.

Read this together with the ticket. G40-271 (opened Apr 12 2026, Highest, still In Progress) documents the exact mechanism: after the March 2026 sign-up enhancement, a user who reaches the email OTP screen and does not finish is shown that screen forever — the back arrow refreshes in place, and there is no way to correct the email or resend the OTP. The ticket's own words: they are completely stuck. The data shows the consequence: 4,247 of the 4,525 email-blocked gophers (94%) signed up before March 2026 — precisely the "existing users with unconfirmed email addresses" population the ticket describes.

Email verification jumps from ~42% to ~88% in March–April 2026 and payout verification rises with it (~20% → ~30%). That is a real, sustained improvement for new signups — and it is not a maturation artifact: older cohorts have had far longer to verify and sit far lower. The cost is that the same release stranded the entire legacy unconfirmed-email population behind a gate they have no way to pass.

5. What is NOT the cause

  • Not Android. iOS and Android reach payout verification at an identical 26.8%. The known Android onboarding complaints are real, but they are not what gates payout.
  • Not the date-of-birth bug. Accounts with a pre-1970 DOB verify at 21.1% vs 24.3% for normal DOBs — and actually verify their email at a higher rate. Only 15 accounts carry an impossible DOB.
  • Not junk or bot signups. 95.9% of blocked accounts use mainstream consumer email domains (3,332 gmail.com alone). These are real, deliverable addresses.
  • Not app abandonment. Only 720 accounts (6.7%) logged in and never opened the worker app, and only 5 never logged in at all.

7. What I would do

  • Re-prioritise G40-13 off "Low". It has been open since August 2024 and sits directly on 2,975 workers who cleared every other hurdle. Gophers are being sent to Stripe's business onboarding and asked for a "business website" that does not apply to them. This is the cheapest unlock on the board.
  • Ship the G40-271 escape hatch first. Of that ticket's seven scenarios, the one that matters most for supply is Scenario 6/7 — back-arrow navigation plus "change email & resend OTP". That alone unblocks 4,525 accounts.
  • Then run a re-activation campaign, not a recruitment campaign. 7,500 reachable stalled gophers versus 2,532 currently payout-verified — fixing the funnel is worth more than roughly tripling the existing verified base, and costs no acquisition spend. Do not send it until the fixes ship, or it lands people straight back into the same dead end.
  • Sequence by market. The reachable pool lines up with the zero-fulfillment markets from the cluster study — Seattle, Baltimore, Minneapolis, St. Louis and New Orleans each have 55–140 stalled workers against markets with real demand and zero completed deliveries.

Method & caveats

  • Source: Dashboard/data/master/Users.csv (60,689 gopher-role accounts after exclusions). Cohort = gopher-role, created in the last 18 months, not deleted/deactivated (10,757 accounts).
  • Test/internal accounts excluded (@gophergo.io, "test", "placeholder") plus johnnewbury@sbcglobal.net — same rule as the earlier research sessions.
  • Funnel stages are inferred from account state, not event logs: "opened the worker app" = gopher_device populated; "payout-verified" = Gopher Stripe Verified. There is no per-event timestamp for Stripe verification in this export, so time-to-verify cannot be measured — updated_at is not maintained (100% same-day) and was discarded.
  • The email→payout relationship is established by (a) tenure-controlled rates — email-unverified accounts under 90 days old verify at literally 0.0% across all three age buckets — and (b) the monthly trend to zero above. It is a structural gate, not a correlation with engagement: blocked and verified accounts log in at similar rates.
  • Causal attribution to the March 2026 release rests on G40-271's own root-cause statement plus the step change in the cohort data; no deploy log was inspected.
