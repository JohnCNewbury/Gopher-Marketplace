How to read this

  • Usage counts all orders placed (including cancelled/expired) — it measures demand and engagement. Lifetime spend counts delivered orders only (grand total).
  • Classification: minimum 4 orders in the 12-month window. Rising = recent half ≥1.5× prior and +3 orders (or brand-new with ≥4). Declining = recent half ≤50% of prior and −3 orders. Stable = recent half within 0.7–1.4× of prior, active in both halves.
  • Rankings: risers by biggest absolute gain, decliners by biggest absolute drop, stable by 12-month volume.
  • Excluded: internal/test accounts (@gophergo.io, placeholder, “test” emails; 1,124 orders) and John’s personal account (johnnewbury@sbcglobal.net — 3,543 lifetime orders; it would top the stable list).
  • The 12-mo trend sparkline plots each user’s monthly orders Jul 2025–Jun 2026; the dot marks June 2026.
