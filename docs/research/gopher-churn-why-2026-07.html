Why the gophers quit

Research session 4 · the supply side of the scissors · gophers active Jul–Dec 2025 who never delivered again, vs those who stayed · companion to sessions 1–3

Headline: they weren’t fired, rated down, or banned — the math just doesn’t work. Of 382 gophers who delivered in H2 2025, 235 (62%) never delivered again in 2026. Both groups carry 5.0★ ratings; 212 of the 235 never even closed their accounts. The difference is work volume: the typical quitter found 1.3 deliveries a month at ~$4.30 each and walked after 47 days. Gopher churn and requestor churn (session 2) are the same disease — demand too thin per gopher, supply too thin per requestor — seen from opposite sides.

What this says, across all four sessions

  • The flywheel is running backwards in thin markets. Requestors leave because orders expire (session 2); gophers leave because orders are too few (session 4). Every market below critical density loses both sides simultaneously.
  • Earnings are the lever, density is the mechanism. $4.30/delivery × 1.3 deliveries/month isn’t an income, it’s a curiosity. No recruiting message fixes that; only concentrated order flow does. Focusing growth spend on the Raleigh DMA + the five win-back markets (session 3) beats spreading it nationally.
  • Fix week-one, not year-one. 47% of gophers never deliver twice. A first-week experience target (e.g., 3 deliveries in 7 days via priority routing for new gophers) attacks the washout curve directly.
  • Re-activation list is ready: 212 ex-gophers with open accounts (plus 14 deactivated), almost all 5★-rated — message = “order volume in your area is up X% since you left” where session 1 shows it’s true.
  • Caveat on earnings figures: GOPHER EARNINGS in Orders.csv is the per-order payout column; it excludes any cash tips and predates current pricing (suggested-offer NC pricing went platform-wide 2026-07). If payout policy changed recently, the historical medians understate today’s offer — worth restating in any recruiting copy.

Method

  • Churned = delivered ≥1 order Jul–Dec 2025, zero deliveries Jan 1 – Jul 14, 2026. Retained = delivered in both periods. Internal/test gopher accounts excluded.
  • Earnings = the GOPHER EARNINGS column on delivered orders (net payout per order). Monthly figures divide lifetime earnings by months with ≥1 delivery, so idle months don’t deflate them.
  • Ratings received from Ratings.csv (rated_id = gopher). Washout curve covers all 2,027 gophers with ≥1 lifetime delivery, all-time.
  • Exit month = a gopher’s last-ever delivery, censored after Apr 2026 (later months can’t distinguish quit from pause).
