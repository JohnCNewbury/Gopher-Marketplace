G40-306 · Banner Notifications

Gopher Banner Notifications.

One collapsed push-banner template, two brands. Gopher Request / Connect banners carry the full Gopher character; Gopher Go banners carry the “GO” mark lifted from the Gopher Go logo. Everything below is production copy from ticket G40-306 — every trigger, tap-target, and sound group is labelled. Tap any live banner (or the play buttons) to see the slide-in.

Anatomy of the template

  • App icon .gbn-ico — 42 px squircle. Request = white tile + full Gopher character. Go = navy tile + green “GO”.
  • Title .gbn-title — Nunito 800, Midnight Blue, single line.
  • Subtitle .gbn-sub — DM Sans, cool grey — the “Select here to…” action line.
  • Timestamp .gbn-time — “now”, quiet grey.
  • Frosted white card, 20 px radius, soft Midnight-Blue shadow; slides in from the top edge.
  • Sound + on-tap destination come from the G40-306 deck; the New/Legacy toggle only swaps the sound.
