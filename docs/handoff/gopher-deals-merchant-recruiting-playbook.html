1 The offer — why a merchant says yes

Lead every conversation with these. They are the actual Gopher Deals value props — keep the language consistent everywhere.

  • Your customer — Your profit. Deals are redeemed by you — we just make it easy through web viewing. Customers pay you directly.
  • One honest price. Same price in-store and on Gopher — no inflating prices to cover platform fees.
  • No pay-to-play. You're seen by every Gopher user, anytime they're within 25 miles of you — so it's a lot more than just the local population.
  • You keep your brand and your customers. If a customer becomes a loyalty member of yours when redeeming your deal, that's your new customer. Your customer data is yours alone.
  • Gopher handles the last mile. Every Deal can parlay into a Gopher delivery at no cost to you. We'll own the last mile from pick-up to drop-off.
  • Free to pre-register. It takes ~2 minutes and there's no charge to get registered.
💡 One-line pitch: "List your deal on Gopher, reach thousands of nearby neighbors, keep 100% of what you charge, and we handle the delivery — free to join."

10 Handling common questions

Is it really free?

Yes — pre-registration is free and there are no lead fees, no bidding wars, and no commissions on your deals. You keep 100% of the amount you charge for every deal you complete.

Do I keep my customers and my brand?

Yes. Your logo, tagline, and voice stay front and center, and customers order on your own site. Gopher puts you in front of nearby neighbors — it doesn't flatten you into a thumbnail.

What's the catch / how do you make money?

There's no per-order commission on your deal. Gopher earns on the last-mile delivery side (small customer-paid fees), not by taxing your sale — so you can publish one honest price, the same in-store and on Gopher.

How do orders actually work?

Customers browse local deals, view yours, and order directly on your site. If they want it delivered, the order can parlay into a Gopher request and a nearby Gopher brings it — at no cost to you.

I have more than one location.

No problem — you add each location in your owner dashboard and post a deal per location. Each deal (and its pickup) is tied to that specific location.

I'm a service provider, not a shop.

Eligible local pros can offer a defined-price service deal within a radius you choose (up to 50 miles). Customers redeem it and it routes a request directly to you.
