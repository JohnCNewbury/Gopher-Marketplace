What you're really choosing. Not four options — one question, asked twice. Is the unit of this screen a job or a person? Object-led (A, B) is safer, works before anyone has accepted, and maps cleanly onto Connect where volume is real. Person-led (C, D) is braver and is the brand thesis out loud, but both have a hole in the pre-acceptance state, which is exactly when you most need to act.

If you want my read: A with B's byline folded in. The Work Order is the only direction whose distinctiveness is already paid for by data you hold — the milestones, the route, the self-rewriting “Gopher's choice” line — and the Lead Story's treatment of “nobody has accepted yet” is better than anything the others do with that state. C is the one I'd fight for if you want the braver answer, and it is the right shape for Connect. D is not a rival — it is a resting state any of the other three could adopt.
