The problem being fixed. The worker’s offer on 65171 was $30 and the goods came to $10.14 after tax. The app shows the requester $10.14; the payout email and the timeline show $40.14. Both are correct and neither says which it is — so a support agent quoting “it went from $40.00 to $40.14” is talking past a customer looking at fourteen cents on ten dollars.
Why label the timeline instead of changing the app. Changing the app’s numbers to match needs a store release. The label ships today, and the acceptance criterion allows either framing “or the difference is labelled.”

Not changing: the updated_amount column keeps its existing unlabelled shape (From $40.00 to $40.14) — 2,033 production rows carry it and it renders elsewhere. Historical rows are never rewritten; the 2,799 bare Cost Adjustment Requested rows keep showing, matched by prefix.
