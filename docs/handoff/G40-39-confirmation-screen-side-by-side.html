Poll on a short bounded window instead of one shot. Say "checking" rather than showing nothing, and warn on Confirm rather than blocking it — this file's own rule is that a photo hiccup must never block confirmation. After the window with nothing found, the section stays hidden (the Gopher legitimately skipped).

Why C warns instead of blocking

The client cannot tell "the Gopher skipped" from "still uploading." photo_requirement is computed by helpers/completion_photo_policy.js but returned only to the Gopher's app on order update — the requester never receives it, and it describes whether the photo step runs, not whether a photo was taken. With no such signal, a hard block would strand every requester whose Gopher legitimately skipped. A bounded poll plus an explicit warning is honest about what is and isn't known.
