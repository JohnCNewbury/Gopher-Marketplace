Merchant Pre-Registration: How It Works

The concise tour of the Deals recruiting pipeline — from the first post a merchant sees, to a pin on our coverage map, to the already-built approval console, to production.

LIVE — gopher-deals.netlify.app
1

Pre-registration is launched

Outreach is live across email, social, SMS, and in-person flyers — anchored by a 4-slide social carousel (the hook → who it’s for → try your address → your real reach). Every message carries a channel-tagged link or a scan-tracked QR, so each sign-up reports exactly which channel produced it. The merchant pitch is simple and free: your customer, your price, your profit — Gopher handles the last mile.

The carousel’s hook slide (QR is scan-tracked)
The live page — gopher-deals.netlify.app
2

Registration → our coverage map

The merchant completes a validated 2-minute form. Each submission is automatically geocoded (exact map coordinates) and channel-stamped, then lands in a live intake sheet with an instant email alert.

The registration form
The intake sheet — geocoded, channel-stamped, with review status
The Raleigh DMA coverage tracker in the Gopher HQ Dashboard

The sheet uploads into the Gopher HQ Dashboard — our internal command center, which already runs company-wide analytics, moderation, and Gopher iQ, and now includes a dedicated Deals section. There, every merchant becomes a pin on the Raleigh DMA map, so we watch coverage build in real time and aim recruiting at the gaps. The same data drives follow-ups on outstanding requirements (notably each merchant’s vector logo for a crisp in-app presence).

🎯 Coverage standard: a minimum of 20 merchants per category within any 15-mile radius, across the entire Raleigh DMA.
3

The approval console is already built

Inside the HQ Dashboard’s Deals section, the full deal-review console is built and running ahead of launch: every deal carries a Deal ID and flows through a governed lifecycle — Considered (new submissions the admin can edit, contact the merchant about, then approve or reject) → Pending (approved, staged for release) → Live, with expired and rejected deals archived below. When the Deals platform ships to production, the approved queue becomes the launch inventory — day one, customers see real local merchants, not an empty shelf.

The Deals admin console in the HQ Dashboard — Live & Pending deals above, the approval queue below (demo data until the live feed connects)
4

Steady state — built to convert to full automation

After launch, the interim intake sheet retires. Merchants register directly through the merchant portal on Gopher Deals; each deal flows straight into the Dashboard’s Considered queue; on approval it joins the weekly production batch — a controlled, quality-gated cadence of new deals going live.

Today (pre-launch)
Outreach→Pre-registration page→Intake sheet→HQ Dashboard · map + console→Launch inventory
At production
Merchant portal→Considered queue→Approve→Weekly production batch intake sheet retired
⚙️ Designed for scale from day one. Every manual step in today’s process is a deliberate placeholder for its automated twin: the intake sheet becomes a direct portal-to-database feed, the sheet upload becomes an automatic sync, map pins place themselves the moment a merchant registers, and the weekly batch becomes a scheduled release pipeline. The workflow, the review console, and the quality gates don’t change — only the manual transport gets automated. That’s what lets the same process run at 50 merchants or 5,000.
